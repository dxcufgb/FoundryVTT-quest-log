# Changelog

## 0.6.2

- Optional integration with Dxcufgb's Debug Recorder: when it is installed and the GM records, the quest log adds a
  snapshot of its state (counts of quests, rewards and notes by status, store and upload state, open windows, the
  Familiar Faces & Places integration, caught error codes) and breadcrumbs at its main actions. No titles, text, names,
  image paths or URLs are ever included. Without the recorder nothing changes.
